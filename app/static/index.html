A third attempt: combinatorial lexicon expansion

Instead of only seeding words literally seen in training or in the fixed word list, this generates every root+affix combination the lexicon's grammar allows (e.g. every noun root with every noun suffix) and adds all of them as extra guaranteed single-token vocabulary entries too.
